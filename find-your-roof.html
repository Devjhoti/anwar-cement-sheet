Choose length and colour

Match the sheet length to the rafter span so joints are few and cutting is minimal.

1

Measure the slope

Measure from ridge to eave along the slope, and the total length along the eave. Add an eave overhang so rain clears the wall.

2

Pick the length

Choose from 2 to 10 RFT. A slope of about 2.4 m suits a single 8 RFT sheet; longer slopes use two rows with an end overlap.

3

Pick the colour

Step 4

Before you buy

A short checklist that avoids most roofing problems.

  • Roof slope is enough for drainage (steeper in heavy-rain districts)
  • Purlin spacing matches the sheet length and expected wind load
  • Side overlap of one corrugation, end overlap of 150–200 mm
  • Fixings go through the crest of the corrugation, never the valley
  • Ridge ventilation planned for cattle and poultry sheds
  • Sheets stored flat, dry and off the ground until fixing
  • A trained installer from an Anwar dealer does the fixing
