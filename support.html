Installation

Get it right the first time

General good practice for fibre cement roofing. Your installer will adapt it to the building and local wind and rain conditions.

1

Structure and slope

Check purlin spacing against the sheet length and wind load. Keep enough slope for drainage; increase it in heavy-rain districts.

2

Lay against the wind

Start at the eave on the side away from the prevailing wind so the side laps face away from driving rain.

3

Overlap

Side overlap of one corrugation. End overlap of 150–200 mm, more on shallow slopes.

4

Fix on the crest

Drill, do not punch, the holes. Fix through the crest with hook bolts and sealing washers at every purlin. Do not over-tighten.

5

Ridge and edges

Cap the apex with ridge panels. Leave a ventilation gap on animal sheds. Seal around pipes and edges.

6

Handling and safety

Carry sheets on edge, store flat and dry off the ground. Never walk on unsupported sheets; use crawl boards.
