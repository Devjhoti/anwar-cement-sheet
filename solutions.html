Livestock welfare

DuraRoof boosts livestock welfare

Figures published in the Anwar DuraRoof brochure for dairy and poultry sheds roofed with DuraRoof.

Dairy

  • In summer, the temperature inside the shed reduces up to 12°C, which helps heat stress-related diseases in cattle. Medication costs are reduced up to 25%.
  • Milk and meat production increases up to 35%, and expenses decrease up to 40%.
  • Controlling the temperature in winter keeps a comfortable temperature inside the shed.
  • No harmful gases are produced in the shed, allowing the cattle to stay comfortable and maintain normal food intake and digestion.
  • The natural temperature in the shed prevents reproductive hormone loss and fetus loss.

Poultry

  • Egg and meat production increases up to 35%, and expenses decrease up to 30%.
  • Controlling the temperature keeps the shed cool in summer, reducing medication costs up to 25%.
  • Maintaining the temperature inside the shed during the winter keeps it warm.
  • It prevents the noise of rain, ensuring egg production and chicken health, and maintains a comfortable environment inside the shed.
