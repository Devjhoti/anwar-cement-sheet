Why it works

Built for residential home

Cooler interior

Fibre cement transfers less heat than metal, so rooms stay more comfortable in summer.

Quieter in rain

The dense sheet dampens monsoon rain noise for a calmer home.

Long service life

Resists rust, rot and termites; needs very little maintenance.

Installation tips

Get it right the first time

  • Keep a minimum roof slope of 15° for proper drainage.
  • Side overlap of one corrugation and end overlap of 150–200 mm.
  • Fix on the crest of the corrugation, never in the valley.
  • Use a trained installer from your nearest Anwar dealer.

Recommended products

  • Corrugated roofing sheet – 6ft, 8ft, 10ft
  • Ridge panel for the roof apex
  • J-hooks, bolts, washers and sealing accessories
  • Colour choice: Red, Green, Grey, Natural
