The company

The largest single-line cement sheet manufacturer in the world

Anwar Cement Sheet Ltd. (ACSL) is the fibre cement roofing concern of Anwar Group of Industries, one of Bangladesh’s oldest business houses.

ACSL was incorporated in 2013 and runs a single production line built on German technology, described by the company as the largest of its kind in the world. Around 1,250 people work across manufacturing, quality control, distribution and dealer support.

The plant manufactures to ISO-393:1964 and BDS 1046:1983 and is approved by the Bangladesh Standards and Testing Institution (BSTI). Every sheet is a 6-layered fibre cement product, 4 mm thick, in lengths from 2 to 10 RFT, in natural grey or factory-coloured red, blue and green.

ACSL sits inside the group's Building Materials Division alongside Anwar Cement, Duraroof, Anwar Galvanizing, Anwar Ispat and A1 Polymer, so the roof, the structure beneath it and the fixings can all come from one trusted source.

Standards and procedures followed in production of DuraRoof

Anwar Group of Industries has established the largest cement sheet factory in the world. DuraRoof is manufactured in two state of the art manufacturing plants of 500 TPD capacity each, equipped with imported machinery using Italian technology.

Sustainability

Making sense of a durable and sustainable roofing solution

Bangladesh is at high risk for extreme heat hazards due to its tropical climate, and the risk is rising over time. Renovating and retrofitting industrial sheds with cement sheets can be a long-term and sustainable cooling system for factories.

Using fossil fuel-generated electric air conditioning creates a vicious circle of greenhouse gases. More air conditioning leads to higher emissions, resulting in a warmer climate, which is again treated by installing more air conditioning. Heat conditions inside factories can be improved with suitable building structures and passive cooling systems.

  • Reduce absence rates and productivity loss
  • Sustainable composition
  • Mitigate temperature up to 12 degrees Celsius
  • Increase production in livestock (poultry and dairy) and reduce farm operating expense
  • Reduce physical and psychological impacts of heat stress, ensuring employee wellbeing
