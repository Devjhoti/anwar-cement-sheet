Farm roofing

A roof for animal comfort

Heat stress lowers milk yield, growth and egg production. A fibre cement roof keeps the shed cooler and quieter, so cattle and poultry stay calmer and more productive.

Get Quote Call hotline 16685

Why it works

Built for animal husbandry

Lower shed temperature

Less heat transfer than metal sheets keeps animals comfortable in the afternoon.

No condensation drip

Cement sheet breathes, reducing condensation that drips on animals and feed.

Corrosion-proof

Unaffected by ammonia and humidity inside cattle and poultry sheds.

Installation tips

Get it right the first time

  • Plan ridge ventilation so hot air escapes at the top.
  • Keep eave height at least 3 m for airflow in cattle sheds.
  • Overhang the roof 600 mm beyond the wall to keep rain out.
  • Ask your dealer for a shed layout check before buying.

Recommended products

  • Corrugated roofing sheet – 8ft and 10ft lengths for wide spans
  • Ridge panel with ventilation gap option
  • Fixing accessories for timber or steel purlins
  • Natural grey finish reflects more sunlight
